## Açıklama (EN) — English Description

Keligo is the most enjoyable Turkish word-guessing game — learn while you play!

Guess the hidden Turkish word by selecting letters from the keyboard. Each correct letter reveals itself, each wrong guess brings the hangman closer. Find the word before 6 mistakes!

🎮 7 GAME MODES
• Daily Word & Calendar — One word per day, everyone in Turkey solves the same puzzle. Revisit past days on the calendar!
• Infinite Mode — 21 categories (Animals, Cities, Music, Sports, Technology, History, Mythology and more), endless fun
• Chapter Mode — 20 progressive levels, earn stars, advance
• Speed Mode — How many words can you solve in 30, 60 or 90 seconds?
• Kids Mode — Short and easy words, ad-free, family-friendly
• Weekly Challenge — 7 days, 7 words, special completion rewards
• Challenge a Friend — Create a code with your own word, share it, let them guess!

💡 5 SMART HINTS
Clue sentence · Reveal vowels · Reveal a letter · Undo last guess · Skip word

❤️ LIVES SYSTEM
Play with 5 lives. Lose one per game loss, refill every 30 minutes. Run out? Watch an ad, spend coins, or wait!

🏆 34 ACHIEVEMENTS & XP
Keep your streak, earn XP, level up. Milestone rewards and streak bonuses make every game count!

🇹🇷 FULL TURKISH SUPPORT
Special keyboard for Ç, Ğ, İ, Ö, Ş, Ü. Hand-curated database of 1500+ Turkish words.

## Xcode'da Kalan Manuel Adımlar

1. **Signing & Capabilities → App Groups**: `group.com.fozzylabs.keligo` ekle
2. **Signing & Capabilities → iCloud**: Key-value storage etkinleştir
3. **Signing & Capabilities → Game Center**: Etkinleştir
4. **Signing & Capabilities → Push Notifications**: Etkinleştir
5. **Info → URL Types**: `keligo` scheme ekle (Arkadaşa Sor deep link)
6. **App Store Connect**: Yukarıdaki 12 IAP ürününü oluştur
7. **App Store Connect**: Game Center leaderboard'larını oluştur:
   - `keligo_tr_speed_leaderboard` (Hız Modu Skoru)
   - `keligo_tr_xp_weekly` (Haftalık XP)
8. **Privacy Policy'yi** web'e yükle → URL'yi App Store Connect'e ekle
9. **Screenshots** çek ve yükle
10. **Archive → Distribute** ile TestFlight'a gönder, test et, sonra App Store'a submit et
